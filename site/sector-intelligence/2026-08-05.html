7How to Read This Report

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A weighted composite: 30% trend/price structure, 25% relative strength vs SPY, 20% macro tailwind/headwind, 15% news & catalysts, 10% momentum/breadth. +100 = maximum bullish evidence; −100 = maximum bearish. Zero means no clear edge.
Conviction (High / Med / Low)
How consistent is the evidence? High = all five inputs point the same way. Low = conflicting signals or thin data. A score of +60 with High conviction beats +80 with Low conviction for swing traders.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Technically-led score asking: "which stock is most likely to move strongest in the sector's direction over 3 days–6 weeks?" 35% setup quality, 25% relative strength, 20% catalyst/earnings proximity, 20% beta/liquidity. Drives stock selection.
Fundamental Health (0–100)
Independent of the chart — absolute business quality. 20% revenue growth, 20% EPS growth & beat history, 15% margins, 15% FCF & balance sheet, 10% analyst revisions, 10% guidance/backlog, 10% dividends/buybacks. Does not pick direction; grades quality of the underlying business.
⛽ Fuel Tag — what it means
For longs: Health ≥65 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL (fundamentals support the move) · 45–64 = NEUTRAL · <45 = ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (chart and fundamentals disagree — more risk).
For shorts: Health ≤40 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak fundamentals agree with the short) · 41–60 = NEUTRAL · >60 = ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (healthy company = riskier short — say so).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag
If a company's next earnings report falls within your swing hold window (~3 days – 6 weeks), it's flagged ⚠ amber. Earnings reports create binary moves that can gap past stops. Consider: sizing down, exiting before the report, or not entering at all. A clean (✅) flag means earnings are outside the window.
