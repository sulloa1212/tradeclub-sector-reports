7How to Read This Report

A quick legend — the generic method behind the numbers. The actual results are in the sections above; this is just how to interpret them.

Direction Score (−100 … +100)
A weighted composite of 5 inputs: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth (10%). Positive = net bullish signals; negative = net bearish. Zero means genuinely mixed — not a pick.
