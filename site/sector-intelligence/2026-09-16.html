7How to Read This Report

This legend explains the methods used — the actual results and their plain-English reasons are in the sections above.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)

A weighted composite of five inputs: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). Scores are the analyst's judgment, not a mechanical formula — they reflect the balance of evidence at run time.

BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL Label

Score > +20 = BULLISH (green). Score < −20 = BEARISH (red). Score −20 to +20 = NEUTRAL (grey). These are direction labels for the swing horizon (3 days to 6 weeks), not long-term investment ratings.

Conviction (High / Medium / Low)

How many of the five scoring inputs agree. High = most inputs aligned in the same direction. Low = inputs conflict and the call is more tentative. When conviction is Low, the report says "no clean setup" rather than forcing a pick.

Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)

For individual stocks: how likely is the stock to move hardest in the sector's direction over the swing window? Weighted on technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst (20%), and move-strength potential (20%). This drives stock selection — pick the best mover, not the best business.

Fundamental Health Score (0–100)

Separate from the swing score — rates absolute business quality: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). Color: green ≥65 / amber 45–64 / red <45.

⛽ Fuel Tag

For longs: Health ≥65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (fundamentals back the chart). 45–64 → NEUTRAL. <45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (weak business; higher risk). For shorts/avoid: Health ≤40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (fundamentally weak company). >60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (shorting a strong company — higher risk, as confirmed for NVDA and ASML).

⚠ Earnings Inside Window

If a company reports during the 3-day–6-week swing window, the earnings date is flagged in amber. Earnings create binary overnight gaps that can invalidate a technical setup entirely. Always check the confirmed earnings date before entering — never rely solely on memory or AI estimates.
