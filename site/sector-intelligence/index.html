7How to Read This Report

This section explains the scoring methods — the rules behind the numbers. The actual results (scores, picks, ratings) all live in the sections above.

Direction Score (−100 to +100) A single number summarizing how bullish or bearish a sector looks for swing trading. It is computed from five weighted inputs: Trend & Price Structure (30%), Relative Strength vs SPY (25%), Macro Tailwind/Headwind (20%), News & Catalyst Flow (15%), and Momentum/Breadth (10%). +100 = max bullish; −100 = max bearish; 0 = perfectly neutral.
Signal Labels BULLISH = score roughly +30 or higher. NEUTRAL = roughly −29 to +29. BEARISH = roughly −30 or lower. These are directional reads, not buy/sell signals.
Conviction (High / Med / Low) Measures how many of the 5 scoring inputs agree. High = 4–5 inputs pointing the same direction. Medium = 3 aligned. Low = mixed signals. High conviction means the read is more consistent; Low means the analysis could flip easily on new data.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) A per-stock score measuring how strongly that stock is likely to move in the sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Driven by: Technical Setup Quality (35%), Relative Strength (25%), Catalyst & News (20%), Move-Strength Potential — beta, range, liquidity (20%). This score drives which stocks are SELECTED and ranked.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) A separate, chart-independent score measuring business quality: Revenue Growth (20%), EPS Growth & Beat History (20%), Margins (15%), FCF & Balance Sheet (15%), Analyst Revisions (10%), Guidance/Backlog (10%), Dividend/Capital Returns (10%). Non-payers reallocate that last 10% to growth & FCF. Color: green ≥65 / amber 45–64 / red <45.
⛽ Fuel Tag Tells you whether fundamentals support or fight the swing-trade direction. For longs: Health ≥65 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL · 45–64 = NEUTRAL · <45 = ⚠ FIGHTS TREND. For shorts/avoid: Health ≤40 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL · 41–60 = NEUTRAL · >60 = ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (a healthy company is a riskier short — we say so explicitly).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag If a stock's next earnings date falls within the 3-day–6-week swing window, it gets an amber ⚠ flag. Earnings are binary events — they can instantly invalidate a technical thesis. Always know your earnings date before entering a swing trade.
