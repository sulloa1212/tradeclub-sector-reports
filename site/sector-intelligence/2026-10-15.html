7How to Read This Report

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
This is the sector's overall bullish or bearish tilt. It combines five weighted inputs: ETF trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth indicators like RSI (10%). +80 = strong bullish; −55 = strong bearish; 0 = neutral, no clean setup.
Conviction: High / Medium / Low
How confident the score is. High = multiple signals aligning clearly (e.g., Energy today). Medium = a real directional edge but conflicting signals present. Low = the direction is tentative; treat as a watch, not an action. If the tape is choppy and conviction is Low, the right answer is "no clean setup" — we say that rather than manufacturing fake conviction.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
This is a stock-level score asking: "Which stock is most likely to move hardest in its sector's direction?" It is technically-led (35% technical setup, 25% relative strength, 20% catalyst/earnings, 20% beta/move potential). A score of 80+ means very strong alignment; 50–65 = decent but not dominant; below 50 = the setup is not clean enough to act on.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
This is completely separate from the Swing-Conviction score. It measures business quality: revenue growth, EPS growth & beat history, margins, free cash flow, balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance, and capital returns. It does NOT tell you which direction the stock will move — it tells you how strong the business is. Use it to size risk: a healthy business makes a worse short.
⛽ Fuel Tag
For long setups: Health ≥ 65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (strong business reinforces the bullish swing). Health 45–64 → NEUTRAL. Health < 45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (weak business is a risk for a long).
For short/avoid setups: Health ≤ 40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak business makes the short safer). Health 41–60 → NEUTRAL. Health > 60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND — shorting a healthy business is higher risk; the short can work, but you need tighter discipline.
⚠ Earnings-In-Window Flag
Any earnings date that falls within the swing horizon (roughly 3 days to 6 weeks from today = Oct 15 to ~Nov 26, 2026) gets a ⚠ amber flag. Earnings are binary events — they can instantly reverse a technically-perfect setup. Always size smaller into earnings, or close before the print. Dates outside the window are shown in green.
