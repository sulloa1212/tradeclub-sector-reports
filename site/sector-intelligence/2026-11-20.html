7How to Read This Report

This legend explains the method — the results are in the sections above. Read this once to understand all the numbers and labels you'll see in every edition.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number capturing how bullish or bearish a sector looks for swing trading (3 days–6 weeks). Scores are built from 5 inputs: ETF trend (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwinds (20%), news/catalysts (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). +100 = maximum bullish; −100 = maximum bearish; 0 = truly mixed.
Conviction: High / Medium / Low
How much we trust the Direction Score. High = multiple confirming inputs align cleanly. Medium = mixed signals but direction is clear. Low = noisy tape, one dominant factor, or oversold/overbought conditions creating uncertainty.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A stock-level score for how likely a stock is to move hardest in its sector's direction over the swing window. Built from: technical setup (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), and move-strength/beta potential (20%). Higher = stronger conviction to outperform in that direction (long or short). This drives which stocks are selected.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Measures business quality independently of the chart: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/capital returns (10%). This score does NOT tell you which direction to trade — it tells you if the business supports or fights the swing thesis.
