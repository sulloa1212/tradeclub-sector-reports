Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) — How strongly we expect THIS STOCK to move in the sector's direction over a 3-day–6-week swing horizon. This drives stock SELECTION within a sector, not long-vs-short — the sector direction determines that.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) — An independent read on the underlying business quality: revenue growth, EPS/beat history, margins, FCF & balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance/backlog, capital returns.
