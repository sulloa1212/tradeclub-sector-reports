7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every number on this page has a clear definition.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
The sector's overall directional tilt. +100 = maximum bullish; −100 = maximum bearish. Built from 5 weighted inputs: Trend & Price Structure (30%), Relative Strength vs SPY (25%), Macro Tailwind/Headwind (20%), News & Catalyst Flow (15%), Momentum & Breadth (10%). Positive = favors longs; negative = favors shorts or avoidance.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident the model is in that Direction Score. High = multiple inputs agree clearly. Low = conflicting signals; the score reflects a best-guess in a noisy environment.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A stock-level score for how strongly a stock is likely to move in its sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days to 6 weeks). Weighted by: Technical Setup Quality (35%), Relative Strength (25%), Catalyst & News (20%), Move-Strength Potential / Beta (20%). Higher = more likely to move hard and fast. This is the selection driver.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate measure of business quality — revenue growth, EPS/beat history, margins, FCF & balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance, dividends. Independent of the chart. It tells you whether the fundamentals support or fight the technical trade.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
