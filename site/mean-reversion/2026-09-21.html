Reversion-vs-Ruin Filter (the most important rule)

A low RSI means selling was intense — it does NOT mean the stock is safe to buy. Always ask:

  • Is there a structural break? Fraud, bankruptcy, failed trial, going-concern — these are not reversion setups. The stock is being re-rated permanently.
  • Is the drop enormous with no stabilization? A −50%+ move in days with no halt/bounce is a falling knife. Wait for a floor.
  • Is it a buyout pin? Post-merger-announcement stock prices are pinned to deal value — there is no mean to revert to.
  • Does the business remain fundamentally intact? The cleanest setups are sound businesses hit by sentiment/positioning excess, ideally near known support levels.

ETF-Specific Caveats

  • K-1 tax flag: USO, UNG, UGA, UCO, BOIL, DBC issue a Schedule K-1 at tax time — more complex filing. Prefer PDBC or BCI for broad commodity exposure without K-1.
  • Equity proxy ≠ commodity: GDX/GDXJ track mining companies, not gold. XOP tracks E&P stocks, not crude. These carry equity earnings risk and can diverge sharply from the commodity. Always stated as "proxy" in the tables.
  • Leveraged ETFs (SOXL, UCO, BOIL, TQQQ, etc.): Daily leverage resets cause volatility decay. Use for short-term tactical trades only. Multi-week holds in leveraged ETFs almost always underperform the unlevered index due to compounding losses.
  • Long-vol ETPs (VXX, UVXY): They structurally decay from VIX futures contango. Never buy them on "oversold" readings — that is just decay. Trade them only when stretched UP (spike fade).
  • Trump-Xi Summit (Sept. 24): A confirmed wildcard. Do not size any position above your normal risk threshold going into this event.
