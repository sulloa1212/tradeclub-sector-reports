Conviction (High / Medium / Low) How cleanly the evidence lines up. High = most inputs agree. Medium = mixed signals, some conflicting. Low = noisy tape, weak edge. Use conviction to size positions — Low conviction = smaller size or paper-trade only.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) This is the technically-led score for a single stock, measuring how likely it is to move hard in the sector's direction over a 3-day–6-week swing window. It drives stock selection and ranking. 85+ = very high conviction. Below 60 = marginal setup. It is NOT a measure of business quality.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) A separate business-quality score: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/capital returns (10%). Color: green ≥65 / amber 45–64 / red <45.
