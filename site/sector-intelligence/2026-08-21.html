7How to Read This Report

The methodology behind every score and label — so you know exactly what each number means.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
Weighted blend of: Trend & price structure (30%), Relative strength vs SPY (25%), Macro tailwind/headwind (20%), News & catalyst flow (15%), Momentum/breadth (10%). Positive = bullish lean; negative = bearish lean. Not a price target.
Conviction (High / Med / Low)
How clean and consistent the evidence is across all five scoring inputs. High = multiple inputs agree; Low = mixed signals, lower confidence in the direction call.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
How likely a stock is to move hardest in its sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks). Technically-led: Technical setup 35% · Relative strength 25% · Catalyst/earnings proximity 20% · Beta/move-size potential 20%.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Independent of the chart — absolute business quality: Revenue growth 20% · EPS growth & beat history 20% · Margins 15% · FCF & balance sheet 15% · Analyst revisions 10% · Guidance/backlog 10% · Capital returns 10%.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
For longs: Fundamental Health ≥65 — strong business reinforces the bullish chart thesis. For shorts: Health ≤40 — weak business reinforces the bearish thesis. Highest-conviction setups.
NEUTRAL / ⚠ FIGHTS TREND
NEUTRAL (45–64 for longs; 41–60 for shorts): mixed signal. FIGHTS TREND: a very healthy company is a riskier short (strong earnings can bail it out); a very weak company is a riskier long (fundamentals drag against price). Not a veto — just a caution flag.
⚠ Earnings-in-window flag
Any earnings event falling inside the 3-day–6-week swing window is flagged amber. Earnings can cause 10–30% moves in either direction — size down, use defined-risk structures, or wait for the print. Never guess the direction of an earnings reaction.
Color scheme
Green = bullish/long · Red = bearish/short or avoid · Amber = warning/event risk · Grey = neutral/mixed · Blue = informational. Consistent throughout every section.
