7How to Read This Report

A quick reference for every score, badge, and term used above.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number summarizing how bullish or bearish a sector looks right now. Scored using five inputs — trend (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news/catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). A score above +50 is meaningfully bullish; below −50 is meaningfully bearish; ±20 = roughly neutral.
Conviction: High / Medium / Low
How confident the score is. High = multiple inputs agree (trend, macro, and news all aligned). Low = mixed signals — the score is a lean, not a certainty. Never size as if High conviction = guaranteed outcome.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Technically-led score for individual stocks: how likely is this stock to be the strongest mover in its sector's direction over 3 days–6 weeks? Weighted: technical setup quality 35%, relative strength 25%, catalyst/earnings proximity 20%, move-size potential (beta/liquidity) 20%. This drives stock selection.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality — independent of the chart. Weighted: revenue growth 20%, EPS growth & beat history 20%, margins 15%, FCF & balance sheet 15%, analyst revisions 10%, guidance/backlog 10%, dividend/capital returns 10%. Tells you if the business supports the trade or fights it.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
For longs: Health ≥ 65 — strong business aligns with the bullish setup. For shorts/avoids: Health ≤ 40 — weak business makes the bearish case easier.
NEUTRAL (Fuel Tag)
For longs: Health 45–64 — business is OK but not a tailwind. For shorts: Health 41–60 — not a clean setup, needs more confirmation.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND (Fuel Tag)
For longs: Health < 45 — weak business fighting an uptrend; higher failure risk. For shorts/avoids: Health > 60 — strong business makes it a riskier short; the company can outrun the narrative.
Earnings-in-Window Flag
If a stock reports earnings within the swing window (roughly 3 days–6 weeks from today), it carries outsized binary risk. We flag all such cases.
