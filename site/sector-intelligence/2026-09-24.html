7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every number makes sense — read this once and you'll always know what you're looking at.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)

A composite of five inputs: ETF trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth such as RSI, MACD, and % members advancing (10%). A score of +72 means "strongly bullish based on current evidence." A score of −62 means "strongly bearish." Anything between −20 and +20 is neutral — no strong edge.

Conviction: High / Medium / Low

How much the five scoring inputs agree with each other. High conviction = most inputs point the same way. Low conviction = mixed signals; treat the score with more skepticism. A Low-conviction +28 is less actionable than a High-conviction +72.

Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) on Stock Cards

A technically-led stock-selection score (not the same as Direction). Four inputs: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & news including earnings proximity (20%), and move-strength potential/beta (20%). A score of 88 means "strongest candidate to move hardest in the sector's direction over 3 days–6 weeks." A score of 64 means "has edge but less clean." Higher = more confident the setup delivers a big move. This drives which stocks we pick, not which we like as long-term investments.

Fundamental Health Score (0–100) on Stock Cards

An absolute business quality score, independent of the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividend/capital returns (10%). For non-dividend payers, the 10% dividend weight is reallocated to growth & FCF. This is NOT the same as "should I buy it" — it simply tells you whether the company's fundamentals are working with or against the price direction. A bearish thesis on a healthy company (health ≥65) is inherently riskier.

⛽ Fuel Tags

⛽ ADDS FUEL — Fundamentals point the same direction as the trade (health ≥65 for longs; ≤40 for shorts). The setup has both technical and fundamental wind behind it.

NEUTRAL — Fundamentals are neither a big boost nor a major drag.

⚠ FIGHTS TREND — Fundamentals push back against the trade direction. A healthy company is a riskier short; a weak company is a riskier long. Reduce size or use defined-risk options.

⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag

Any stock reporting earnings within the 3-day–6-week swing horizon carries this flag. Earnings are the #1 source of gap risk — the stock can move 10–40%+ overnight in either direction regardless of your setup. Options: (a) size smaller, (b) sell or reduce before the print, (c) use defined-risk options strategies, or (d) wait for the post-earnings reaction to set up a trade. Never hold a full swing position through earnings without a defined plan.
