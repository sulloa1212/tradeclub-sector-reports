7How to Read This Report

A quick guide to what every number and label means — so you always know what you're looking at.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A weighted composite reading for each sector: +100 = maximum bullish conditions, −100 = maximum bearish. Weights: Trend & price structure 30%, Relative strength vs SPY 25%, Macro tailwind/headwind 20%, News & catalyst flow 15%, Momentum & breadth 10%.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident we are in the direction read. High = multiple aligned inputs, clear evidence. Low = some supporting signals but meaningful noise or counter-arguments. Never trade Low-conviction signals as if they are High.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for how strongly a name is likely to move in its sector's direction. Technically-led: 35% technical setup, 25% relative strength, 20% catalyst & earnings proximity, 20% move-strength potential (beta/range). Higher = stronger expected move.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
