Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score (not sector) for how strongly we expect THIS stock to move in the sector's direction, based on: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & news (20%), move-strength potential / beta (20%). Fundamental Health (0–100)

⛽ Fuel Tag
Shows whether the fundamentals HELP or FIGHT the technical direction. For longs: Health ≥ 65 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL (healthy business going up = best case). For shorts/avoids: Health ≤ 40 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak business going down = easier short). ⚠ FIGHTS TREND means you're fighting the fundamentals — higher risk, tighter stops.
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag
Our swing horizon is 3 days–6 weeks. If a company reports earnings inside that window, we flag it in amber. Earnings prints are binary events — they can move a stock 5–20% in one session, wiping out or dramatically amplifying any swing thesis. Always know your earnings date before entering a swing position.
