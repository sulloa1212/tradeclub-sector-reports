7Options Structures Legend

This legend maps the direction and IV environment to educational defined-risk structures — always paper-trade first and confirm data at your brokerage.

OVERSOLD + HIGH IV → SELL PREMIUM
  • Bull put spread: Sell a put, buy a lower put. Profit if stock stays above short strike. Best when IV is elevated (you collect more credit).
  • Cash-secured put: Sell a put and hold cash to buy shares if assigned. Income strategy — you want to own the stock at the lower price.
  • Why high IV helps: Elevated IV means options are more expensive, so you collect more premium when you sell.
OVERSOLD + LOW IV → BUY OPTIONS
  • Bull call spread: Buy a call, sell a higher call. Defined upside, defined cost. Use when IV is low (options are cheap to buy).
  • Long call: Simple bullish bet on a bounce. Higher risk — don't use when IV is elevated (you'd overpay).
OVERBOUGHT + HIGH IV → SELL PREMIUM
  • Bear call spread: Sell a call, buy a higher call. Profit if stock stays below short strike. Defined risk — maximum loss is the spread width minus credit received.
  • Why "defined risk first": A naked short call has unlimited theoretical loss. The long call caps it.
OVERBOUGHT + LOW IV → BUY OPTIONS
  • Bear put spread: Buy a put, sell a lower put. Profit if stock falls. Lower cost than a straight long put.
  • Long put: Direct bearish bet. Use only when IV is low so you don't overpay for the option.
WHAT THE INDICATORS MEAN
  • RSI (14-day): 0–100 scale. <30 = oversold (sold too hard, may bounce). >70 = overbought (ran too far, may pull back). Not a guarantee — just a flag.
  • Bollinger %B: Where price sits within its 20-day volatility band. <0 = below the band (stretched down). >1 = above the band (stretched up).
  • Z-score: How many standard deviations (typical daily moves) price is from its 50-day average. Beyond ±2 is statistically unusual.
  • vs 50d / 200d SMA: How far price is from its average over 50 or 200 days. Large negative % = stretched to the downside.
IV RANK GUIDE
  • IV elevated (high): Options are expensive. Favor selling strategies (spreads, cash-secured puts) to collect inflated premium.
  • IV low: Options are cheap. Favor buying strategies (debit spreads, long calls/puts) — you're not overpaying for the option.
  • GVZ: Gold's implied volatility index — spiked +10.7% Mon; confirms elevated IV on GLD/SLV/GDX.
  • MOVE: Bond market's implied volatility index — at 101.82, highest in years; confirms elevated IV on TLT/TBT.
