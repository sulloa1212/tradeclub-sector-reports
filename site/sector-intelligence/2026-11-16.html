7How to Read This Report

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number summarizing how bullish or bearish a sector looks right now. Inputs: trend vs ETF's own moving averages (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwinds/headwinds (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth — RSI, MACD, % of members trending up (10%).
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident the scoring is in the direction call. High = most inputs agree clearly. Low = mixed signals or limited data. Low conviction means the score could flip quickly — treat with extra caution.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Scores how likely an individual stock is to make the BIGGEST move in its sector's direction, over a 3-day to 6-week swing horizon. Technically-led: setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), beta/move-potential (20%). This drives stock SELECTION.
