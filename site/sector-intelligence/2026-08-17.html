7How to Read This Report

This legend explains the methodology behind the scores — not the conclusions themselves (those live in each section above).

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A composite read on a sector's swing-trading direction. Weighted inputs: Trend & price structure (30%), Relative strength vs. SPY over 1–3 months (25%), Macro tailwind/headwind (20%), News & catalyst flow (15%), Momentum/breadth — RSI, MACD, % members up — (10%). Positive = bullish lean; negative = bearish lean. Zero = no clear edge.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How much agreement there is across all five inputs. High = 4–5 inputs aligned. Medium = 3 aligned. Low = 2 or mixed. Low conviction means the sector may be range-bound or choppy — trade smaller or skip.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for HOW HARD a stock is likely to move in its sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days – 6 weeks). Weighted: Technical setup quality (35%), Relative strength (25%), Catalyst & news / earnings proximity (20%), Move-strength potential — beta, range, liquidity — (20%). Higher = expected to be a bigger mover. This is the stock-selection driver — it is NOT a measure of how "good" the business is.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality, independent of the chart. Weighted: Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), Margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), Analyst revisions (10%), Guidance/backlog (10%), Dividend/capital returns (10%; reallocated to growth & FCF for non-payers).
