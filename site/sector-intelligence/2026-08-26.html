7How to Read This Report

A quick legend explaining the methodology behind the scores and symbols — the "why the numbers look the way they do."

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A composite score for each sector, not a single stock. Weighted inputs: Trend/price structure 30% · Relative strength vs SPY 25% · Macro tailwind/headwind 20% · News & catalyst flow 15% · Momentum & breadth 10%. A score of +78 means the weighted inputs strongly favor bulls; −50 means the inputs strongly favor bears.
Conviction (High / Med / Low)
How consistent the signals are across the five inputs. High = all or nearly all inputs agree. Low = mixed signals; trade carefully or wait for confirmation.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A technically-led score for an individual stock — how likely it is to move hardest in the sector's direction. Inputs: Technical setup quality 35% · Relative strength 25% · Catalyst & news 20% · Move-strength potential (beta/range) 20%. High score = strongest expected mover in that direction. This is the main tool for picking which stocks to focus on.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A SEPARATE, chart-independent score measuring business quality. Inputs: Revenue growth 20% · EPS growth & beat history 20% · Margins 15% · FCF & balance sheet 15% · Analyst revisions 10% · Guidance/backlog 10% · Dividend/capital returns 10% (reallocated to growth/FCF for non-payers). This score tells you if the company's business supports or fights the chart setup.
⛽ ADDS FUEL / ⚡ NEUTRAL / ⚠ FIGHTS TREND
The "Fuel Tag" connects the Fundamental Health score to the trade direction. For longs: Health ≥65 = ADDS FUEL (strong business behind the setup). For shorts/avoid: Health ≤40 = ADDS FUEL (weak business supports the bear case). In either direction, FIGHTS TREND means the fundamentals cut against the chart — a riskier trade.
⚠ Earnings-In-Window flag
A stock with earnings inside the swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks) carries event risk — the stock can move +/− significantly on the print regardless of the chart setup. Options market "implied move" tells you the expected post-earnings range. Consider sizing smaller, using options to define risk, or waiting for the post-earnings reaction before entering.
Colors: green / red / amber / blue
Green = bullish / long bias. Red = bearish / short-or-avoid bias. Amber = caution / mixed / event risk. Blue = neutral informational. Consistent across all bar charts, score numbers, and tags.
