7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every number makes sense — the methodology behind the scores.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A composite of five weighted inputs: ETF trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs. SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth — RSI, MACD, % members rising (10%). A score of +58 means the five inputs collectively lean bullish; −52 means collectively bearish. The score is TODAY's read — it can change as data changes.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How clearly the five inputs agree. High = 4–5 inputs point the same direction. Low = inputs are split and the score could flip quickly.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for how likely a stock is to MOVE HARDEST in the sector's direction over a 3-day–6-week swing window. Weighted: Technical setup quality 35%, Relative strength 25%, Catalyst & news 20%, Beta/range/liquidity ("move-strength") 20%. A higher score = a cleaner, stronger expected move — it says nothing about the size of the move or whether you should trade it.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate, independent assessment of the company's business quality (not its chart). Factors: Revenue growth 20%, EPS growth & beat history 20%, Margins 15%, FCF & balance sheet 15%, Analyst revisions 10%, Guidance/backlog 10%, Dividends/buybacks 10% (reallocated to growth & FCF for non-payers). Health and Swing-Conviction AGREE when both point the same direction; they CONFLICT when a technically weak stock is fundamentally strong (risky short).
⛽ Fuel Tag
