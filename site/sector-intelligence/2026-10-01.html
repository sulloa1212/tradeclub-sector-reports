7How to Read This Report

This section explains the method — not the results. All result numbers (scores, dates, levels) are in the body above.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A weighted composite of 5 inputs: ETF trend/price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth — RSI, MACD, member breadth (10%). A score of +50 means meaningfully bullish on balance; −50 means meaningfully bearish. The center (0) is flat/mixed with no clean setup — we say so rather than manufacturing conviction.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How much agreement there is across all 5 inputs. High = at least 4 of 5 pointing the same way and the macro/technical story is consistent. Low = mixed signals where one or more inputs conflict with the direction score — trade smaller or skip.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for how likely a name is to move hardest in the sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Inputs: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & news (20%), move-strength potential — beta, range, liquidity (20%). A higher score does not mean "safer" — it means higher expected movement, in either direction.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality, independent of the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/capital returns (10%). Color grading: green ≥65 · amber 45–64 · red <45.
⛽ Fuel Tag
Shows whether the fundamental health adds to or fights the directional trade. For longs: Health ≥65 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL · 45–64 = NEUTRAL · <45 = ⚠ FIGHTS TREND. For shorts/avoids: Health ≤40 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL · 41–60 = NEUTRAL · >60 = ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (a healthy company is a riskier short).
⚠ Earnings-In-Window Flag
Any stock with a confirmed or estimated earnings date within the 6-week swing horizon gets a warning. Earnings inside the window mean the stock can move violently against your position regardless of the technical setup — size down, hedge, or wait for the print to clear. If a date is marked "unconfirmed — verify," do not assume the date shown is correct.
