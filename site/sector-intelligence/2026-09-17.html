7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every number and label makes sense — this section explains the method, not the results.

Direction Score (−100…+100)
A weighted composite for each sector: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth (10%). Positive = net bullish evidence; negative = net bearish. Zero = genuinely mixed — no strong edge.
Conviction (High/Medium/Low)
How confident the weighted inputs are in agreement. High = most inputs point the same way. Low = inputs are mixed and the score is more tentative. Low conviction means the setup is real but noisier — size positions accordingly.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
The technically-led score for individual stocks: how likely the stock is to move hardest in the sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks). Inputs: technical setup quality 35%, relative strength 25%, catalyst & news 20%, move-strength potential (beta/range) 20%. This drives WHICH stock we pick.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate, chart-independent score for business quality: revenue growth 20%, EPS growth & beat history 20%, margins 15%, FCF & balance sheet 15%, analyst revisions 10%, guidance/backlog 10%, dividend/capital returns 10%. It does NOT drive selection — it modifies the risk label via the Fuel Tag.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
For longs: Health ≥ 65. Business quality supports the technical trend — lower risk. For shorts: Health ≤ 40. A weak business is a more natural short candidate — lower risk.
NEUTRAL (Fuel Tag)
For longs: Health 45–64. Business quality neither helps nor hurts. For shorts: Health 41–60. Same range — the business is okay, neither adding nor fighting the short thesis.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND
For longs: Health <45. A weak business fights the bullish technical setup. For shorts: Health >60. A very healthy company is a riskier short — fundamentals can overwhelm the technical story. Size down and keep stops tight.
⚠ Earnings in Window
If a company reports earnings during the 3-day–6-week swing horizon, the position carries binary event risk — the stock can gap dramatically in either direction. Treat this as an amber flag: either size down before the date, exit before earnings, or choose a different name.
Color Code
Green = bullish / long. Red = bearish / short or avoid. Amber = elevated risk or mixed signal. Blue = informational / neutral emphasis. Applied consistently throughout the report.
