7How to Read This Report

A quick glossary so every number on this page makes sense.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)

How bullish or bearish a sector is RIGHT NOW, on a scale from −100 (maximum bearish) to +100 (maximum bullish). Zero = perfectly neutral. Calculated from 5 inputs: trend (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwinds (20%), news/catalysts (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). A score above +50 or below −50 signals high conviction. The score is subjective and based on available data — treat it as a structured opinion, not a fact.

Conviction (High / Medium / Low)

How confident we are in the Direction Score, given data quality and number of confirming signals. High = multiple independent signals align (trend, macro, news all pointing same way). Low = signals conflict or data is sparse. A High conviction bearish score is not necessarily "more bad" than Low — it just means we have better evidence for our read.

Swing-Conviction Score (0–100 on stocks)

How likely a specific stock is to move STRONGEST in its sector's direction over a 3-day to 6-week swing horizon. Technically led: setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), move-strength potential / beta (20%). A score of 80+ = very high conviction. NOT a buy/sell signal — a setup quality gauge for YOUR research.

Fundamental Health Score (0–100 on stocks)

How healthy the BUSINESS is, independent of the chart. Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), capital returns (10%). Green ≥65 = strong business. Amber 45–64 = mixed. Red <45 = weak. A high health score on a bearish pick means the BUSINESS is fine but the STOCK is overvalued or out of trend.

⛽ Fuel Tags

Tells you whether fundamentals amplify or resist the swing thesis. ⛽ ADDS FUEL = fundamentals support the move (good for longs, or weak fundamentals for shorts). NEUTRAL = mixed signals. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND = the swing direction and the business quality conflict — extra caution warranted. A short on a stock with "FIGHTS TREND" is riskier because you're fighting a good business.

⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag

An amber ⚠ warning when a stock's next earnings date falls inside the ~3-day to 6-week swing window. Earnings create binary outcomes — large gaps up or down — that can overwhelm any technical setup. Options or smaller size are appropriate. Always verify the exact earnings date against your brokerage before trading, as AI can misstate dates.
