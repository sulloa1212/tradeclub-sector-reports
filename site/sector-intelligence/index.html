7How to Read This Report

This section explains the methodology — how the scores are built and what each label means. The actual scores and conclusions are in Sections 3–5.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A weighted composite score for each sector. +100 = maximum bullish evidence across all five inputs; −100 = maximum bearish. Scores near 0 are genuinely neutral. Built from: Trend & price structure (30%), Relative strength vs. SPY (25%), Macro tailwind/headwind (20%), News & catalyst flow (15%), Momentum/breadth — RSI, MACD, member %, (10%).
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How consistent the evidence is across all five inputs. High = all 5 inputs agree. Medium = 3–4 agree. Low = mixed or thin data. A High-conviction +40 beats a Low-conviction +70 for trading purposes — alignment matters more than magnitude.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Answers "which stock will move hardest in the sector's direction?" Built from: Technical setup quality (35%), Relative strength (25%), Catalyst & news proximity (20%), Move-strength potential — beta, range, liquidity (20%). Higher = the report expects the stock to move most in that direction over 3 days to 6 weeks.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Separate from the chart — grades the underlying business: Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), Margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), Analyst revisions (10%), Guidance/backlog (10%), Dividend/capital returns (10%). For non-dividend payers, that 10% is reallocated to growth & FCF.
⛽ Fuel Tag
Shows whether the fundamental picture helps or fights the chart direction. For longs: Health ≥65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL; 45–64 → NEUTRAL; <45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND. For shorts/avoids: Health ≤40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak company = easier short); 41–60 → NEUTRAL; >60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (healthy company = riskier short).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag
Appears on any stock with a confirmed or likely earnings date within the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Earnings = binary gap risk — the stock can move ±5–10% overnight. Always verify the exact date with your broker before holding through a print. If the date is unconfirmed, the report says so explicitly rather than guessing.
Color Guide
Green (bull-c / t-bull) = bullish / long thesis. Red (bear-c / t-bear) = bearish / short-or-avoid. Amber (warn-c) = caution / binary risk / elevated uncertainty. Grey (mut) = neutral / no clean read.
