Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A composite of five inputs weighted as follows: ETF trend & price structure (30%) — above rising 20/50-EMA with higher highs/lows is bullish; Relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%) — outperformance is bullish; Macro tailwinds/headwinds (20%) — rates, dollar, commodities favoring the sector; News & catalyst flow (15%) — earnings tone, policy; Momentum/breadth (10%) — RSI, MACD, % members above key MAs. Scores are directional reads for the swing horizon, not price targets.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How clear and consistent the signals are across the five inputs. High = most inputs agree; Low = conflicting signals, or limited data. Lower conviction = smaller position sizes.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for how likely the name is to move HARDEST in the sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks). Weights: Technical setup quality (35%), Relative strength (25%), Catalyst & news (20%), Move-strength potential / beta (20%). Higher = stronger setup, not a higher share price.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality, independent of the chart. Weights: Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), Margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), Analyst revisions (10%), Guidance/backlog (10%), Dividend/capital returns (10%). Non-payers: the 10% dividend weight is reallocated to growth & FCF.
⛽ Fuel Tag (Longs)
Health ≥ 65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (strong business supports the long). Health 45–64 → NEUTRAL. Health < 45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (weak business working against the long).
⛽ Fuel Tag (Shorts/Avoid)
Health ≤ 40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak business supports the short). Health 41–60 → NEUTRAL. Health > 60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (healthy company = riskier short — said explicitly in the card).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag
Amber warning when a stock's next earnings date falls within the swing window (roughly 3 days–6 weeks from today). Earnings create large gap risk — a setup can be technically perfect and still move against you 10%+ on the print. Size down or avoid holding through earnings unless that is intentional.
