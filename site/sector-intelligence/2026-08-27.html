Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A sector's overall bullish or bearish lean, built from 5 weighted inputs: trend/price structure (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). A score above +50 = strong bullish; below −50 = strong bearish; within ±25 = choppy/neutral. Scores are opinion-based estimates, not mathematical certainties.
Conviction: High / Medium / Low
How confident the analysis is in the score. High = multiple inputs align cleanly. Medium = some conflicting signals. Low = the read is tentative — data is thin or the setup is early. When conviction is Low or Medium, prefer smaller sizing or waiting for confirmation.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A stock-level score for how likely that name is to move hardest in its sector's direction over a 3-day to 6-week swing window. Built from: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & earnings proximity (20%), and move-strength potential / beta (20%). This score drives stock SELECTION — higher = more likely to lead the move.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality, independent of the chart. Built from: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividend & capital returns (10%). This score says nothing about whether the stock moves — it says whether the business underneath is strong or weak. Use it alongside the Swing-Conviction score, not instead of it.
⛽ Fuel Tags
Shows whether the fundamental health reinforces or contradicts the directional bet. For long setups: Health ≥65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (strong business behind the bullish chart); 45–64 → NEUTRAL; <45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (chart bullish but business is weak — higher risk). For short/avoid setups: Health ≤40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak business reinforces bearish thesis); 41–60 → NEUTRAL; >60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (fundamentally healthy company is a riskier short).
