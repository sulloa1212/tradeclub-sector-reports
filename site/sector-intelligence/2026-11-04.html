7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every number on this page has a clear meaning.

Direction Score
−100 to +100 How bullish or bearish a sector looks right now. Computed from 5 inputs: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs. SPY over 1-3 months (25%), macro tailwinds/headwinds (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth like RSI (10%). A score of +70 means the evidence heavily favors more upside; −60 means significant downside pressure. Zero = no clean read.
Conviction
High / Med / Low How clearly the evidence points in the scored direction. High = multiple inputs align; Low = mixed signals, one dominant factor. Lower conviction = wider error bars on the score.
Swing-Conviction Score
0–100 For individual stocks: how strongly this stock is likely to move in its sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks). Technically led (35% chart setup, 25% relative strength, 20% catalyst, 20% beta/range). Higher = more conviction the move will be fastest and largest. It is NOT a measure of safety or quality — just momentum potential.
Fundamental Health
0–100 Absolute business quality, independent of the chart. Covers revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), free cash flow & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). For longs, high health adds confidence. For shorts, high health is a warning — a good company is a riskier short.
⛽ ADDS FUEL Fundamentals support the swing direction. For longs: health ≥65. For shorts/avoid: health ≤40. The business quality reinforces the technical signal.
NEUTRAL (fuel tag) Fundamentals neither add nor fight the trade. For longs: health 45–64. For shorts: health 41–60.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND Fundamentals push against the swing direction. For longs: health <45 (weak company in a strong sector — fragile). For shorts: health >60 (strong company being shorted — risky, can recover fast). Always tighten stops and size down when this tag appears.
Earnings-in-window flag ⚠ means earnings fall within the swing horizon (roughly the next 6 weeks). Earnings bring binary event risk — the stock can move 5–20% in one session.
