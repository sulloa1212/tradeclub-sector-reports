7How to Read This Report

A quick guide to every score, badge, and signal in this report — what each one measures, how it's calculated, and what range means what.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
The overall directional read for a sector or theme. Combines five inputs: Trend & price structure (30%) — is the ETF above or below rising moving averages?; Relative strength vs. SPY (25%) — outperforming or lagging the market?; Macro tailwind/headwind (20%) — do rates, dollar, commodities favor this sector?; News & catalyst flow (15%) — positive or negative earnings/policy developments?; Momentum/breadth (10%) — RSI, MACD, % of members above key MAs. Scores above +40 = bullish; below −40 = bearish; −40 to +40 = neutral/mixed.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident we are in the Direction Score. High = multiple inputs agree cleanly. Medium = most inputs agree but one conflicts. Low = mixed signals, noisy tape — the direction is a lean, not a strong call.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for how likely that name is to move hardest in its sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days to 6 weeks). Inputs: Technical setup quality (35%) — trend aligned, clean breakout/breakdown, volume?; Relative strength (25%) — leading or lagging vs. peers?; Catalyst & news (20%) — upcoming event or recent driver?; Move-strength potential (20%) — beta, range, liquidity enough to deliver a big move? Higher score = more likely to move strongly in sector direction.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate, independent score of business quality — has nothing to do with the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividend/capital returns (10%). This tells you whether the fundamentals are behind the technical move — or fighting it.
⛽ Fuel Tags
For long ideas: Health ≥ 65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (strong business confirms chart); 45–64 → NEUTRAL (business doesn't hurt or help); below 45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (weak business could cap the move).
For short/avoid ideas: Health ≤ 40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak business accelerates downside); 41–60 → NEUTRAL; above 60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (healthy company is a riskier short).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag
An amber ⚠ flag on a stock card or calendar row means the next earnings date may fall inside the 3-day–6-week swing window. Earnings releases cause large, unpredictable price gaps that can override any technical setup. Always verify the exact date with your broker before sizing a position.
