7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so you know how every number and label was built.

Direction Score (–100 to +100)
The analyst's overall read on a sector's bullish/bearish lean. Built from five inputs: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth including RSI, MACD, % of members trending up (10%). A score of +86 (Energy) means strong evidence across all five inputs pointing bullish. A score of –52 (Homebuilders) means the opposite. Scores near 0 are genuinely neutral — no clean edge.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How consistent the evidence is across all five scoring inputs. High = multiple inputs all agree. Medium = majority agree but at least one pushes back. Low = inputs are mixed or the data is thin.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A stock-level score for how likely that stock is to move strongest in its sector's direction over 3 days to 6 weeks. Technically led: 35% chart setup quality, 25% relative strength, 20% catalyst & upcoming events, 20% beta/range potential. Higher = the analyst expects a bigger move. This drives stock selection within a sector.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality score — independent of the chart. Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), profit margins (15%), free cash flow & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). A high score does NOT mean "buy" — it means the business is healthy. For a bearish/short call, a high fundamental score is a red flag (the company can fight back). For a long, a high score adds confidence.
⛽ ADDS FUEL tag
For long setups: Fundamental Health ≥65 = business quality confirms the bullish chart. For short/avoid setups: Fundamental Health ≤40 = weak fundamentals agree with the bearish chart. NEUTRAL = fundamentals are neither strongly supportive nor strongly contrary. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND = fundamentals disagree with the directional call — trade with extra caution and smaller size.
⚠ Earnings in Window
If a stock's next earnings date falls within the ~3-day to 6-week swing horizon, it is flagged in amber. Earnings create a binary event that can override your setup entirely — the stock can gap 10–20% in either direction regardless of your thesis. Always confirm earnings dates via your broker before entering. When in doubt, reduce position size or wait for the report to pass.
