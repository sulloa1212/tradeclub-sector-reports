7How to Read This Report

A quick legend for newcomers — the methodology behind the numbers you see.

Score & Label Definitions

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A model score for each sector's overall bullish/bearish tilt. Built from five weighted inputs: ETF trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). A score above +50 is strongly bullish; below −50 is strongly bearish; near 0 is neutral with no clear edge.
BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL label
The one-word direction call derived from the score. Conviction (High/Medium/Low) reflects how clean and multi-factor-consistent the signal is — a High-conviction call has most inputs pointing the same way.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A technically-led score for individual stocks within a drilled sector: how strongly is this stock likely to move in the sector's direction over a 3-day to 6-week swing window? Inputs: technical setup (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & news (20%), move-strength potential / beta (20%). This drives stock selection, not fundamental quality.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Separately measures business quality: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), free cash flow & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/capital returns (10%). This is about the business — it can agree with or fight the technical setup.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
