7Options Structures Legend

This legend explains the method — how direction and IV map to defined-risk structures. The specific setups, prices, and tickers are in the tables above. Always paper-trade any structure before risking real money.

Oversold + High IV → Premium Selling (bullish)
  • Cash-secured put: sell a put below market; collect premium; obligated to buy shares at strike if assigned. Best when you'd welcome owning shares at that price.
  • Bull put spread: sell a put, buy a lower put. Defines your max loss to the width of the spread minus premium collected. Preferred when IV is elevated.
  • Bull call spread: buy a call, sell a higher call. Defined cost (the debit you pay). Preferred when IV is lower — reduces cost vs outright call.
Overbought + High IV → Premium Selling (bearish)
  • Call credit spread: sell a call above market, buy a higher call. Defines max loss to spread width minus premium. Profits if stock stays below short strike.
  • Bear put spread: buy a put, sell a lower put. Defined cost (the debit). Preferred when IV is lower — cheaper way to position for a pullback vs outright put.
IV Gauge — Why It Matters
  • High IV (Implied Volatility): options are expensive. Premium selling structures (credit spreads, cash-secured puts) tend to be more efficient — you collect the inflated premium.
  • Low IV: options are cheap. Premium buying structures (debit spreads, outright calls/puts) are more efficient — you pay less for defined risk.
  • Always check IV Rank (how current IV compares to its own 52-week range) in your brokerage platform before choosing a structure.
Technical Term Glossary
  • RSI (0–100): measures speed of recent gains vs losses. Below 30 = oversold (sellers may be exhausted). Above 70 = overbought (buyers may be stretched). Below 20 or above 80 = extreme.
  • Bollinger %B: where price sits within its Bollinger Band envelope. Below 0 = outside the lower band (oversold). Above 1 = outside the upper band (overbought).
  • Z-score: how many standard deviations (a measure of normal variation) price is from its 50-day average. ±2 or beyond = statistically rare.
  • 50d / 200d SMA: the 50- or 200-day Simple Moving Average — the "mean" a stretched price is expected to revert toward.
  • ATR (Average True Range): typical daily price swing. A 3-ATR move in a few days = unusually stretched.

8How to Read This Report & Guardrails

Reversion-vs-Ruin Filter — the most important rule. A low RSI is necessary for a reversion setup, but it is NOT sufficient. This report rejects names where the drop is driven by a structural break (fraud, going-concern risk, failed drug trial, bankruptcy, dividend cut, accounting restatement), a buyout repricing, or a catastrophic -50%+ collapse with no stabilization. Only names where the move looks like sentiment/positioning excess over an otherwise sound business make it through. When in doubt, the report flags the risk and suggests skipping or sizing tiny.
Long-vol ETPs
(VXX, UVXY)
Trade only when RSI is stretched UP on a vol spike — that is the fade. Never buy them on a low RSI; contango roll decay grinds them lower over time. UVXY's 1.5× leverage causes faster contango decay in normal markets.
Inverse-vol ETPs
(SVXY, SVIX)
They grind up in calm markets and crater on vol spikes. Don't fade their up-moves; their reversion setup is the recovery after a vol crash.
Leveraged ETFs
(UCO, TQQQ, TBT…)
Volatility decay and futures roll mean they deviate from any long-run mean. Use only for short-term tactical reversion; for multi-week holds prefer the unleveraged fund or equity-sector proxy.
K-1 Tax Flag
(USO, UNG, DBC…)
These funds issue a Schedule K-1 at tax time, which complicates filing. Prefer no-K-1 alternatives where available: PDBC instead of DBC; BCI or COMB instead of GSG.
Proxy vs Spot
(GDX, XOP, URA…)
These ETFs track companies (miners, drillers, uranium producers), not the commodity price directly. They carry additional equity beta and can diverge significantly from spot prices — especially during broad market selloffs.
Earnings Risk
An upcoming earnings report inside ~30 days is a known binary event that can override any technical setup. The report flags these with ⚠. Always confirm the earnings date in your brokerage before entering a position.
