7How to Read This Report

A plain-English guide to every score and indicator used above.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number summarizing how bullish or bearish a sector looks right now. Built from five inputs: Trend & price structure (30%) — is the ETF above rising moving averages, making higher highs? Relative strength vs SPY (25%) — is it beating the market? Macro tailwind/headwind (20%) — do rates, dollar, and commodities favor it? News & catalyst flow (15%) — positive or negative earnings/policy news? Momentum/breadth (10%) — RSI, MACD, % of members trending up. Higher score = more bullish; lower = more bearish.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How much signal agreement is behind the Direction Score. High = most inputs agree and the trend is clear. Low = mixed signals or a sector in transition where the score could shift quickly. Never trade a Low-conviction read with the same size as a High-conviction one.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Measures how likely a specific stock is to be the strongest mover in its sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Technically led: 35% chart setup quality, 25% relative strength, 20% catalyst proximity, 20% beta/range. A bullish sector + high swing score = best long setups. A bearish sector + high swing score = most vulnerable shorts/avoids.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Measures the underlying business quality — independent of the chart. Revenue growth 20%, EPS growth & beat history 20%, margins 15%, FCF & balance sheet 15%, analyst revisions 10%, guidance/backlog 10%, dividend/capital returns 10%. Tells you whether the stock's momentum has durable business backing.
⛽ Fuel Tag
How the Fundamental Health supports (or fights) the swing direction. For longs: Health ≥ 65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL · 45–64 → NEUTRAL · <45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND. For shorts/avoids: Health ≤ 40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL · 41–60 → NEUTRAL · >60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND — a fundamentally strong company is a riskier short.
⚠ Earnings-In-Window Flag
Any stock with a confirmed or probable earnings report within the 3-day–6-week swing horizon is flagged in amber. Earnings = binary event risk: the stock can gap 10–20% in either direction overnight. Size earnings-window positions smaller. Use defined-risk structures (debit spreads, long options) rather than uncovered positions if you want to hold through a report.
