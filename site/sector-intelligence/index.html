7How to Read This Report

A quick reference explaining the numbers and labels used throughout this page.

Direction Score (−100 to +100) A weighted composite of 5 inputs: trend/price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). Zero = perfectly neutral. The formula is applied identically to every sector every run.
