7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every term and score makes sense on first read.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A weighted composite score for each sector. Inputs: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwinds/headwinds for the sector (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth like RSI and % of members rising (10%). Positive = bullish lean; negative = bearish lean.
Conviction (High/Med/Low)
How strong and clear-cut the signals are. High = multiple inputs align clearly. Low = mixed or uncertain signals. When in doubt, the report says "neutral/no clean setup" rather than forcing a pick.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A technically-led score for individual stocks within a drilled sector. Drives stock selection. Inputs: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength within the sector and vs the market (25%), catalyst & news including earnings proximity (20%), and move-strength potential — beta, range, and liquidity (20%). This tells you which stock is most likely to move hardest in the sector's direction.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
An independent measure of business quality, separate from the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), free cash flow & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). This tells you if the company behind the chart setup is healthy or fragile.
⛽ Fuel Tags (Longs)
⛽ ADDS FUEL = Health ≥ 65: strong fundamentals support the bullish chart setup. NEUTRAL = Health 45–64: neither adds nor subtracts. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND = Health < 45: weak fundamentals create extra risk for a long setup.
⛽ Fuel Tags (Shorts/Avoids)
⛽ ADDS FUEL = Health ≤ 40: weak fundamentals support the bearish thesis. NEUTRAL = Health 41–60. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND = Health > 60: a healthy company is a riskier short — the fundamentals argue against the bearish chart read.
⚠ Earnings-in-Window
If a stock's next earnings date falls within the 3-day–6-week swing horizon, the report flags it amber. Earnings are binary events — the stock can gap sharply either direction regardless of the chart setup. Treat flagged names as higher-risk; size down or use defined-risk options if trading around them. Always verify the date with your own brokerage first.
