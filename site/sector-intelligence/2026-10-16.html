7How to Read This Report

A plain-English guide to the scoring systems used throughout this report.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number showing how bullish or bearish a sector is right now. +100 = maximum bullish signal; −100 = maximum bearish. Calculated from five inputs: ETF trend (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth (10%). A score ≥ +50 = BULLISH; ≤ −50 = BEARISH; between = NEUTRAL.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident the model is in the direction call. High = multiple confirming signals, clear trend. Medium = direction readable but cross-currents exist. Low = ambiguous — could go either way. Low conviction means "lean this way but keep position small."
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
For individual stocks: how strongly the stock is likely to move in the sector's direction over a 3-day–6-week swing horizon. NOT a prediction — a relative ranking. 80+ = very high setup quality; 60–79 = solid; below 60 = interesting but proceed carefully. Built from: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & news (20%), move-strength potential (20%).
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
The business quality of the company, independent of the chart. Built from: revenue growth, EPS growth & beat history, margins, FCF & balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance, and capital returns. This is NOT a trading signal by itself — it tells you whether the fundamentals support or fight the chart direction.
