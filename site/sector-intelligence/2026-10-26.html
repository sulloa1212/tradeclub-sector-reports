7How to Read This Report

A quick legend — the method behind the numbers. The body of the report shows the results; this section explains how they're built.

Direction Score (−100 to +100): A sector's overall bullish/bearish read. Built from: Trend & price structure (30%), Relative strength vs SPY (25%), Macro tailwind/headwind (20%), News & catalyst flow (15%), Momentum/breadth (10%). A score above +40 = meaningfully bullish; below −40 = meaningfully bearish; −40 to +40 = mixed/neutral.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low): How many of the five scoring inputs agree with each other. High = 4–5 inputs aligned. Low = mixed signals, conflicting data.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100): A stock's technical likelihood of moving hardest in the sector's direction over 3 days–6 weeks. Technically led: setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), move-strength potential (20%). This drives WHICH stocks we pick.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100): How strong the underlying business is — separate from the chart. Revenue growth, EPS growth, margins, FCF/balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance, capital returns. A high health score on a bearish pick means the short is riskier — the business fights the technical thesis.
⛽ ADDS FUEL: For a long: health ≥ 65 — fundamentals support the bullish technical setup. For a short/avoid: health ≤ 40 — a weak business makes the bearish case easier.
