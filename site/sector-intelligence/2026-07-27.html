7How to Read This Report

A quick legend for the numbers, scores, and labels you see throughout — methodology lives here, not in the body.

Direction Score
−100 to +100 composite for each sector. Weighted: Trend/price structure 30%, Relative strength vs SPY 25%, Macro tailwind 20%, News & catalyst 15%, Momentum/breadth 10%. Above 0 = more signals bullish than bearish; the magnitude shows how much.
Conviction
High = multiple confirming signals across all 5 factors. Medium = 3–4 factors agree. Low = 1–2 or noisy data. When conviction is Low, score size alone doesn't justify a trade.
Swing-Conviction Score
0–100, technically-led, drives stock selection within a sector. Factors: Technical setup quality 35%, Relative strength 25%, Catalyst & news 20%, Move-strength potential (beta/range) 20%. This is about which stock moves the hardest in the sector's direction — not about the business being "good."
Fundamental Health Score
0–100, independent of the chart. Revenue growth 20%, EPS growth & beat history 20%, Margins 15%, FCF & balance sheet 15%, Analyst revisions 10%, Guidance/backlog 10%, Dividend/capital returns 10% (non-payers: reallocated to growth & FCF). Measures business quality, not short-term price direction.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
Longs: Health ≥ 65 — the business supports the bullish chart (best combo). Shorts/avoid: Health ≤ 40 — weak fundamentals add weight to the bearish case.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND
Longs: Health < 45 — the chart says up but the business is weak (added risk). Shorts/avoid: Health > 60 — the chart says down but the business is strong (harder short — flag to size conservatively).
⚠ Earnings In Window
If a company reports earnings within your swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks), that event is a binary risk — the stock can gap significantly in either direction. Reduce size, use defined-risk structures (if options-literate), or wait for the report. Never enter full size into an earnings date blindly.
AVOID / FADE vs SHORT
In this report, bearish picks on energy names are labeled "Avoid / Fade" — not short recommendations. This means: reduce existing positions, don't add new longs, or consider tactical hedges. An outright short on a company with Fundamental Health ≥ 65 (⚠ FIGHTS TREND) carries additional risk and requires strict risk management.
