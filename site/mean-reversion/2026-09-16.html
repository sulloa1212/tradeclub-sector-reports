7Options Structures Legend

This section explains the general educational logic for each structure. The specific results — which names to apply them to, at what strikes — are in the tables above. Always paper-trade and verify data before risking real money.

📈 Oversold + High IV → Put Credit Spread

Sell an out-of-the-money put, buy a further out-of-the-money put. Collects premium (high IV helps). Profit if the stock stays above the short put at expiration. Max loss is the spread width minus credit received. Defined-risk.

📈 Oversold + Low IV → Bull Call Spread

Buy a call near the current price, sell a call further out. Pays a net debit. Low IV makes buying options cheaper. Profit if the stock rises above the long call strike. Max loss = the debit paid. Defined-risk.

📉 Overbought + High IV → Call Credit Spread

Sell an out-of-the-money call, buy a further out-of-the-money call. Collects premium. Profit if the stock stays below the short call at expiration. Max loss is the spread width minus credit received. Defined-risk.

📉 Overbought + Low IV → Bear Put Spread

Buy an at-the-money put, sell a further out-of-the-money put. Pays a net debit. Profit if the stock falls. Max loss = the debit paid. Defined-risk.

🔒 Cash-Secured Put (for oversold, high IV)

Sell a put option and set aside enough cash to buy 100 shares if assigned. Collects premium; you're willing to own the stock at that price. Works best on names you'd be comfortable holding if assigned. Not appropriate for speculative / high-risk tickers.

📊 RSI — What It Measures

0–100 scale. Above 70 = overbought (buying may be exhausted). Below 30 = oversold (selling may be exhausted). Extreme readings: above 80 or below 20. RSI alone is insufficient — confirm with at least 2 more signals.

📊 Bollinger %B — What It Measures

Tells you where price sits relative to its Bollinger Bands. Above 1 = price outside the upper band (extreme overbought). Below 0 = price outside the lower band (extreme oversold). Confirms RSI signals.

📊 Z-Score — What It Measures

How many standard deviations price is from its 50-day mean. Above +2 = statistically stretched high. Below −2 = statistically stretched low. A Z-score of ±3 is rare and historically tends to revert.

8How to Read This Report & Guardrails

Before using any setup in this report, read every item below. These are the filters that separate a reversion trade from a losing trade.

The Most Important Rule: Reversion ≠ "Just Buy the Dip"
A low RSI tells you a stock is stretched. It does NOT tell you it will bounce. You need a reason — the selling was sentiment-driven, not fundamental. Always ask: "Is this a stretched rubber band, or a broken company?"
🚫 Reject these setups (falling knives, not setups):
  • Fraud, accounting restatements, going-concern doubt — RSI does not mean-revert on structural breaks.
  • Failed drug trials, patent cliffs, bankruptcy filings — a -60% move in days is usually not a bounce candidate.
  • Buyout/merger repricing — the stock is pinned to a deal price, not its mean.
  • Permanent regime change (e.g., a commodity that moved on a decade-long structural shift, not an event).
⚠ Leveraged & futures-based ETF caveats:
  • TBT, TQQQ, SQQQ, UCO, SCO, BOIL, KOLD, UVXY, UVIX — daily-reset leverage means volatility decay erodes value over time. Use for short-term tactical trades only; not multi-week holds. Prefer the unlevered fund for longer holds.
  • USO, UNG, DBC, BOIL, CORN — these issue a Schedule K-1 tax form. More complex tax reporting than a standard 1099. Prefer no-K-1 alternatives (PDBC, BCI, SCO) where available.
⚙️ Proxy vs. spot ETF distinction:
  • GDX, GDXJ, XOP, OIH, URA, COPX, MOO — these track companies, not the commodity itself. They carry equity-market beta. They can diverge significantly from the underlying commodity price (especially during risk-off sell-offs when equity beta overwhelms commodity price action).
📅 Earnings & events — ALWAYS check before entering:
  • An upcoming earnings report can override any technical setup. Check your brokerage earnings calendar before every trade.
  • Today specifically: LEN earnings after close, Fed decision at 2 PM ET. Many setups in this report become binaries until those events pass.
  • Every date in this report that came from a live search is noted. Dates marked "unconfirmed — verify" were not found in today's searches. Do not rely on any date in this report without checking your brokerage.
📏 Three-measure rule (how to score a setup): A name extreme on RSI only = weak candidate. A name extreme on RSI + Bollinger %B outside bands + price more than 2× ATR from 20-day SMA = stronger candidate. Three or more confirming signals from: RSI, %B, Z-score, distance from 50/200-day moving average, consecutive directional days — that is a quality setup. Every top-ranked name in this report passed at least three measures.
📌 Always paper-trade first: Options involve substantial risk. You can lose more than your initial investment on uncovered positions. Every structure in this report is defined-risk (spreads), but they can still expire worthless. Paper-trade any new strategy before committing real capital.
