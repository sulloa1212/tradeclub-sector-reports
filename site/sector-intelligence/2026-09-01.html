7How to Read This Report

A quick guide to every score, badge, and term you see — the methodology behind the numbers, not the conclusions (those live in the sections above).

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
Weighted blend: Trend/price (30%), Relative strength vs SPY (25%), Macro tailwinds (20%), News & catalysts (15%), Momentum/breadth (10%). Positive = net bullish; negative = net bearish.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How many of the five inputs agree. High = 4–5 factors aligned. Low = mixed signals, thin data, or volatile regime where the score could flip quickly.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
How likely this specific stock is to move hardest in its sector's direction. Technically-led: trend alignment (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst (20%), beta/range/liquidity (20%).
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality, independent of the chart. Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/returns (10%).
⛽ ADDS FUEL
For longs: Health ≥ 65 — strong fundamentals amplify the technical trend. For shorts/avoids: Health ≤ 40 — weak fundamentals make the bearish case easier to sustain.
NEUTRAL (Fuel tag)
Health 45–64 (longs) or 41–60 (shorts). Business is middling — neither a tailwind nor a strong headwind for the swing direction.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND (Fuel tag)
For longs: Health < 45 — weak fundamentals could undermine a technical rally. For shorts: Health > 60 — a healthy company is a riskier short; mean reversion risk is higher.
Earnings-In-Window ⚠
If a stock's next earnings date falls inside the swing horizon (roughly 3 days to 6 weeks from today), it's flagged amber. Earnings = binary event risk that can override any technical setup.
▲ PICK / ▼ PICK (Sector Board)
The top-2 bullish sectors and top-2 bearish sectors that receive full drill-downs. All other rows are scored but not drilled.
