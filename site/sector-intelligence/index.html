7How to Read This Report

Direction Score (−100 to +100) How bullish or bearish each sector is, based on five weighted inputs: ETF trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth like RSI & MACD (10%). Conviction (High / Medium / Low) How confident we are in the direction score. High means multiple inputs agree strongly. Low means mixed signals or limited data.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) Applies to individual stocks — how likely a stock is to move hardest in its sector's direction over 3 days to 6 weeks. Technically led (trend, base, momentum = 35% weight). Higher = more conviction. For short/avoid picks, higher means more vulnerable.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) Separate from the swing score — rates the underlying business quality (revenue growth, EPS growth, margins, FCF, balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance, capital returns). 0 = distressed; 100 = exceptional quality. Tells you whether the fundamentals support or fight the technical trade.
⛽ ADDS FUEL For longs: Fundamental Health ≥65 — the business is strong enough to support the bullish technical thesis. For shorts: Health ≤40 — the weak business adds to the bear case.
NEUTRAL (fuel tag) Fundamentals are neither a strong tailwind nor a headwind to the trade direction. Health 45–64 for longs; 41–60 for shorts.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND (fuel tag) For longs: Health <45 — the business is weak enough that it may not be able to sustain a rally. For shorts: Health >60 — a healthy company is a riskier short because it may find buyers on dips. Always noted explicitly.
⚠ Earnings In Window The stock reports earnings inside the report's swing horizon (3 days – 6 weeks from today). This is flagged in amber because earnings create binary event risk — the stock could move 5–15% in either direction on the report, regardless of the technical setup.
