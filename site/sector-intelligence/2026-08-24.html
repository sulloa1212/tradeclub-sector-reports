1The 60-Second Read

This is a yield-sensitive tape: sectors that love low rates (real estate, utilities) are being punished; sectors that love AI capex and rising energy prices (semis, energy) are the winners.
  • 🟢 Standout bull — Semiconductors (SMH): The runaway leader of 2026, up roughly 59–65% YTD, with NVIDIA reporting Wednesday (Aug 26) after close — a potential sector-defining catalyst. AI infrastructure spending from hyperscalers remains the clearest fundamental story in the market right now.

  • 🟡 Cushion/risk — Oil at ~$85 WTI: The US-Iran war and Strait of Hormuz partial closure remain the biggest macro wildcard. Oil pulled back ~2% this morning on progress with the Omani shipping route, but the underlying supply shock is still live and could re-escalate. Energy (XLE) benefits as long as oil stays elevated, but a peace deal would flip that trade instantly.
  • ⚠️ Watch this week: Three events could move the tape violently — NVDA earnings (Wed Aug 26 AMC), Fed Chair Warsh at Jackson Hole (Fri Aug 28), and July PCE inflation data (Fri Aug 29). Warsh's debut keynote is genuinely unpredictable: the FOMC is split 9-3 (three regional presidents want to hike) and markets price ~1-in-3 odds of a September rate hike. A hawkish surprise would hammer rate-sensitive sectors and growth stocks alike.
