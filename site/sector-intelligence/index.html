7How to Read This Report

Direction Score (−100 to +100)

Inputs: ETF trend vs. moving averages (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth (10%). A score above +30 = leaning bullish; below −30 = leaning bearish; in between = neutral/choppy.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident the model is in the score — not how big the move will be. High conviction = most inputs agree. Low conviction = conflicting signals. A High-conviction Neutral is still neutral — don't force a trade.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for how strongly a name should move in its sector's direction over a 3-day to 6-week swing window. Led by technical setup (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), and move potential / beta (20%). Higher = more likely to move hardest in the sector's direction.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
How healthy the underlying business is — separate from the chart. Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), capital returns (10%). Does not predict the near-term price move — it contextualizes the risk of the swing trade.
