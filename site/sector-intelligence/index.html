7How to Read This Report

This legend explains the method — the results and their plain-English reasons are in the body above.

Direction Score (−100 … +100): A weighted composite of five inputs — trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). Zero = neutral; +100 = maximum bullish; −100 = maximum bearish.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low): How confident the model is in the Direction Score, based on data quality and signal agreement. Low conviction = conflicting signals or limited data.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100): Stock-level score for how hard a stock is likely to move in the sector's direction over the swing window (3 days–6 weeks). Driven by technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & news (20%), and beta/range/liquidity (20%). This score drives stock selection — it is NOT a "goodness" rating.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100): Absolute business quality, independent of the chart — revenue growth, EPS growth & beats, margins, FCF & balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance, and capital returns. For non-dividend payers, the dividend weight is reallocated to growth & FCF.
⛽ ADDS FUEL: Fundamentals support the swing direction (≥65 for longs; ≤40 for shorts). NEUTRAL: No clear fundamental push. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND: Fundamentals oppose the swing trade (e.g. a healthy business being shorted — size down).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window: The stock's next earnings report falls within the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. This is event risk — the stock could gap violently either direction on the report, regardless of the technical setup. Consider smaller size or defined-risk structures (spreads) around earnings.
Direction Bar (Sector Board): Green bar grows right of center = bullish; red bar grows left = bearish. Bar length = magnitude of the Direction Score.
Color coding: Green = bullish/long. Red = bearish/short-or-avoid. Amber = caution/watch. Blue = informational/neutral context.
