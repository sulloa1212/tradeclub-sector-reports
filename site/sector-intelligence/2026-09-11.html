7How to Read This Report

A quick reference for the scoring systems and symbols used throughout.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
Our overall sector read. Weighted inputs: Trend/price structure (30%) + Relative strength vs SPY (25%) + Macro tailwind/headwind (20%) + News/catalyst (15%) + Momentum/breadth (10%). A +82 means strongly bullish across most inputs; −62 means strongly bearish. Not a price-target — it reflects the current setup quality for a swing-trade horizon (3 days – 6 weeks).
Conviction (High/Medium/Low)
How confident we are in the Direction Score. High = multiple inputs align clearly. Medium = some conflicting signals. Low = early-stage or thin data. A Low conviction score should reduce your position size — the less certain the read, the less you risk.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Stock-level score for how strongly a stock should move in the sector's direction over our swing horizon. Technically-led. Inputs: Technical setup quality (35%) + Relative strength (25%) + Catalyst/news (20%) + Move-strength potential/beta (20%). Higher = stronger expected move. This drives stock SELECTION, not portfolio sizing.
Fundamental Health (0–100)
Separate from the chart — measures absolute business quality: Revenue growth (20%) + EPS growth & beat history (20%) + Margins (15%) + FCF & balance sheet (15%) + Analyst revisions (10%) + Guidance/backlog (10%) + Dividends/capital returns (10%). ≥65 = green (healthy). 45–64 = amber (neutral). <45 = red (weak).
⛽ ADDS FUEL
For long setups: Health ≥65 means the business fundamentals reinforce the bullish technical thesis. For short/avoid setups: Health ≤40 means the fundamentals also point to weakness — the short has both chart AND business support.
NEUTRAL (Fuel Tag)
For longs: Health 45–64 means fundamentals are mixed — the chart is doing the work but fundamentals don't add a tailwind. For shorts: Health 41–60 means you're shorting a reasonably healthy business — be more cautious.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND (Fuel Tag)
For longs: Health <45 means the chart is bullish but the business is weak — beware of reversals. For shorts: Health >60 means you're shorting a healthy company — elevated risk; size smaller and be quick to exit.
Earnings-in-Window flag
If a stock reports earnings during our swing horizon (≈ next 6 weeks), that is an event-risk flag. An earnings release can move a stock 5–20% in one session, overriding any technical setup. Always know your earnings date BEFORE entering a swing trade. When a date is unconfirmed in this report, verify via your brokerage before trading.
