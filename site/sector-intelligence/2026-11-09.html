7How to Read This Report

Quick glossary of every scored element in this report — what each number means and how to use it.

Direction Score (−100 to +100) A weighted composite score for each sector's overall bullish or bearish tilt. +100 = maximum bullish (rising trend, strong relative strength, positive macro tailwind, good news flow, breadth confirming). −100 = maximum bearish. 0 = no clear direction. The five inputs are: ETF trend/price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY 1–3 mo (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth RSI/MACD (10%). This score drives sector ranking on the Sector Board.
Conviction (High / Med / Low) How confident we are in the Direction Score. High = all five inputs align and the signal is clear. Medium = most inputs agree but some ambiguity. Low = mixed signals, noisy tape, or limited data. A Low-conviction Bullish score is NOT a strong buy — it's a "leaning bullish" with significant uncertainty.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) How likely a specific stock is to move the strongest in its sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days to 6 weeks). This is technically-led: 35% technical setup quality, 25% relative strength vs sector & market, 20% catalyst & news (including earnings proximity), 20% move-strength potential (beta, range, liquidity). A higher score means the stock is most likely to move the hardest — it does NOT mean "safer." A 90 on a bearish sector means high confidence this stock falls the most. A 90 on a bullish sector means high confidence it rises the most.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) An independent gauge of business quality — separate from the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). A high score = strong business. For long setups: a high FH score adds fuel to the swing thesis. For short/avoid setups: a HIGH FH score means the company is a great business, which makes it a riskier short — the ⚠ FIGHTS TREND tag warns you.
⛽ Fuel Tags ⛽ ADDS FUEL = fundamentals and swing direction align — both pushing the same way. NEUTRAL = fundamentals are okay but neither confirming nor fighting the swing trade. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND = fundamental direction and swing direction oppose each other. For longs: FH ≥ 65 = ADDS FUEL / 45–64 = NEUTRAL / <45 = FIGHTS TREND. For shorts/avoid: FH ≤ 40 = ADDS FUEL / 41–60 = NEUTRAL / >60 = FIGHTS TREND.
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag Flags when a stock has an earnings release within the swing horizon (roughly 3 days to 6 weeks from today). Earnings are the #1 source of sudden unexpected moves — a stock can move 10–25%+ on earnings, in either direction, overnight. If a name has this flag, factor in the event risk before sizing.
