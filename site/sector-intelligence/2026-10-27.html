7How to Read This Report

A quick legend for every score, meter, and tag used above — this explains the method, not the individual conclusions.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
The report's main sector read. Scored from −100 (maximum bearish) to +100 (maximum bullish) using 5 weighted inputs: trend/price structure (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). Zero = no edge. The bar chart in Section 3 shows all sectors at once so you can see the full spread at a glance.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident the scoring is. High = multiple inputs align cleanly. Medium = setup is real but one or more inputs is mixed or uncertain. Low = interesting but no clean edge yet — don't trade it. Conviction does NOT mean the trade will work — it means the evidence is consistent.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A stock-level score for how strongly a stock is likely to move in its sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Weighted: technical setup 35%, relative strength 25%, catalyst & earnings proximity 20%, move-strength potential (beta/range/liquidity) 20%. Higher = more likely to be the fastest mover in the sector. This score drives stock selection.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Completely separate from the chart — measures absolute business quality. Weighted: revenue growth 20%, EPS growth & beat history 20%, margins 15%, FCF & balance sheet 15%, analyst revisions 10%, guidance/backlog 10%, dividends/capital returns 10% (non-payers: 10% reallocated to growth & FCF). Color scale: green ≥65 = healthy; amber 45–64 = middle; red <45 = weak fundamentals.
⛽ Fuel Tag (ADDS FUEL / NEUTRAL / ⚠ FIGHTS TREND)
Tells you whether the fundamentals support or fight the chart direction. For longs: health ≥65 = "⛽ ADDS FUEL" (fundamentals support the move); 45–64 = NEUTRAL; <45 = "⚠ FIGHTS TREND" (weak business fighting for a bounce — higher risk). For shorts: these thresholds flip — a healthy company (score >60) means "⚠ FIGHTS TREND" (riskier short). A fundamentally weak company (<40) "⛽ ADDS FUEL" to a bearish thesis.
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag
Amber ⚠ on any stock card or calendar entry means an earnings release is expected within the 3-day–6-week swing window. Earnings create event risk — the stock can move 5–15% in either direction regardless of the setup. Always check the exact date; reduce position size into the event; or choose to play the catalyst directly (not recommended for beginners).
