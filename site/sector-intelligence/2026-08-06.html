7How to Read This Report

A quick reference guide to the scores, tags, and methodology used throughout.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
Measures our overall bullish/bearish view on a sector, synthesizing 5 weighted inputs: price trend (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). A score of +62 means meaningfully bullish; −54 means meaningfully bearish. Zero means genuinely mixed — no edge.
Conviction: High / Medium / Low
How confident we are in the directional read. "High" means multiple inputs agree and the picture is clear. "Low" means the signals conflict or the setup is early-stage.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
How likely we think a specific stock is to move HARD in the sector's direction over the next 3 days to 6 weeks. This is a technically-led score — it drives stock selection, not business quality. Inputs: technical setup (35%), relative strength vs market (25%), catalyst/earnings proximity (20%), move-strength potential/beta (20%).
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate, chart-independent measure of business quality. Revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). ≥65 = green / healthy; 45–64 = amber / mixed; <45 = red / weak.
⛽ Fuel Tag — for LONG setups
Health ≥65 → "ADDS FUEL" (strong business supports the move) · 45–64 → "NEUTRAL" (business OK, not a driver) · <45 → "⚠ FIGHTS TREND" (weak fundamentals are a headwind even if the chart looks good).
⛽ Fuel Tag — for SHORT / AVOID setups
Health ≤40 → "ADDS FUEL" (weak company = easier short) · 41–60 → "NEUTRAL" · >60 → "⚠ FIGHTS TREND" (healthy company = riskier short — say so explicitly, as we do for XOM).
⚠️ Earnings-in-Window flag
If a company's next earnings date falls within our swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks from today), it is flagged in amber. An earnings report is a binary event that can gap a stock against your position — size down or wait for the report before entering.
