7How to Read This Report

A quick reference so every number makes sense — bookmark this if you're new.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A weighted composite of trend, relative strength vs SPY, macro tailwinds/headwinds, news flow, and momentum. +100 = maximum bullish conviction; −100 = maximum bearish. This measures the sector's directional lean — it does not predict magnitude of moves.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How strongly the evidence points one way. High = multiple factors aligned clearly. Medium = most factors agree but one is ambiguous. Low = directional lean but mixed signals — smaller position sizes warranted.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A technically-led score for individual stocks estimating the likelihood a stock moves most strongly in its sector's direction over the 3-day to 6-week swing horizon. Higher = stronger setup. This drives which stocks we pick — it is NOT a quality/safety score.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
