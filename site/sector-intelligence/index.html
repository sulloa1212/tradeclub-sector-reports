7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every number makes sense at a glance.

Direction Score (−100 to +100) Shows how bullish or bearish a sector is on a scale from −100 (maximum bearish) to +100 (maximum bullish). Scores are weighted across five inputs: trend/price (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwinds (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). The Sector Board bar chart makes the ranking visual — green bars grow right (bullish), red bars grow left (bearish).
Conviction (High / Medium / Low) How confident we are in the direction score. High = multiple inputs agree and data is clear. Low = conflicting signals or thin data. Low conviction scores may still be correct but should be sized more cautiously.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) A stock-level score for how likely a stock is to move strongest in its sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Technically led: trend (35%), relative strength (25%), catalysts + earnings proximity (20%), beta/move potential (20%). A higher score = more confident the stock will move hardest in that direction.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) Measures the absolute quality of the underlying business, independent of the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). Color: green ≥65, amber 45–64, red <45.
⛽ Fuel Tag For longs: Health ≥65 → ADDS FUEL (strong business supports the chart move). Health 45–64 → NEUTRAL. Health <45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (weak business may limit upside).
For shorts/avoid: Health ≤40 → ADDS FUEL (weak company makes short easier). Health 41–60 → NEUTRAL. Health >60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (healthy company = risky short — say so).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag Flagged in amber when a stock's next earnings date falls inside our ~3-day–6-week swing horizon. An earnings print inside the window is a binary event — the stock can gap 10–30% either direction in a single session. Manage position size accordingly or wait for the print to pass before entering.
