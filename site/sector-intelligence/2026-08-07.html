7How to Read This Report

A quick guide to the numbers and labels used throughout — so you always know what you're looking at.

Direction Score (−100 to +100) The report's overall view of a sector's trend. Combines: ETF trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwinds/headwinds (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth — RSI, MACD, % of members above key MAs (10%).
