7How to Read This Report

A brief legend explaining the methodology behind every score and tag in this report.

Direction Score (−100…+100)
A weighted composite of: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). Positive = bullish setup; negative = bearish. Zero = no clean directional read.
Conviction (H/M/L)
How much of the scoring evidence agrees. High = multiple independent inputs align. Low = conflicting signals; treat with more caution and size smaller.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A separate stock-level score estimating which names will move HARDEST in the sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Weighted: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & earnings proximity (20%), move-strength potential / beta (20%). This drives stock SELECTION. It does NOT measure the quality of the business.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
An absolute measure of business quality, independent of the chart. Weighted: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), capital returns (10%). This is a sanity check — it can support or fight the swing direction.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
For longs: Fundamental Health ≥ 65 — the business quality reinforces the bullish swing case. For shorts/avoids: Fundamental Health ≤ 40 — the business weakness confirms the bear case.
NEUTRAL (Fuel Tag)
For longs: Health 45–64 — business is fine but not a strong confirmation. For shorts: Health 41–60 — business isn't broken, so the trade is primarily technical/macro.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND
For longs: Health < 45 — weak business fighting the bullish setup. For shorts/avoids: Health > 60 — healthy business is fighting the bearish thesis; be cautious sizing short positions. HD and WELL in today's report are examples — good businesses in bad macro situations, making them avoids more than shorts.
⚠ Earnings in Window
Amber warning flag when a stock's next earnings date falls within the 3-day–6-week swing window. Earnings = binary event risk that can gap a stock up or down regardless of your directional thesis. Always verify earnings dates independently before entering any position.
