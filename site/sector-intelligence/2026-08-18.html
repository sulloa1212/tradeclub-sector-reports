A single number summarizing how bullish or bearish a sector looks right now, combining five weighted inputs: price trend (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). A score above +50 is actionable bullish; below −50 is actionable bearish; ±20 is roughly neutral.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident we are in the Direction Score. High = multiple inputs agree clearly. Medium = some mixed signals. Low = the setup exists but the case is weaker or more data-dependent. Never trade a Low-conviction setup at full position size.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Measures how likely a specific stock is to move strongly in the sector's direction over a 3-day to 6-week swing horizon. Technically led: setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), and move-strength potential/beta (20%). Higher = expect a bigger move. This is a movement probability score, not a "good/bad" judgment — a high-beta stock scoring 90 could move 15% either way if the sector thesis reverses.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Measures business health independently of the chart — revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividend/capital returns (10%). Color-graded: Green ≥65 = healthy, Amber 45–64 = mixed, Red <45 = weak.
⛽ Fuel Tag
Combines the Direction Score with Fundamental Health to assess whether fundamentals support or oppose the trade direction.
For LONGS: Health ≥65 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL; 45–64 → NEUTRAL; <45 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (weak fundamentals are a headwind).
For SHORTS/AVOID: Health ≤40 → ⛽ ADDS FUEL (weak company = easier short); 41–60 → NEUTRAL; >60 → ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (a strong company is a riskier short — say so).
⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag
An earnings date that falls within our swing horizon (roughly 3 days to 6 weeks from today = Aug 18 to ~Sep 29, 2026) is flagged in amber. Earnings inside the window add binary event risk — the stock can gap violently in either direction regardless of your technical thesis. Reduce position size or consider options strategies when an earnings date is inside the window.

Red = bearish / short-or-avoid / weak.

Grey = neutral / unconfirmed / informational.
