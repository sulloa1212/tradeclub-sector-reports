7Options Structures Legend

This section explains the method — what the indicators mean and which educational structures fit which situation. The body of the report has the actual findings; this is the how-to reference.

RSI (14) A 0–100 momentum gauge. Below 30 = "oversold" (selling may be exhausted); above 70 = "overbought" (buying may be exhausted). Extreme readings: below 20 or above 80. Always confirm with a second indicator.
Bollinger %B Measures price position within Bollinger Bands. Below 0 = price is outside the lower band (stretched bearish); above 1 = outside the upper band (stretched bullish). Confirms RSI extremes.
Z-Score How many standard deviations price sits from its 50-day average. Above +2 = overbought; below −2 = oversold. A Z-score extreme on top of RSI extreme is high-confidence.
IV (Implied Volatility) The market's guess at future price movement, baked into option prices. High IV = options are expensive → selling premium (credit spreads) is more favorable. Low IV = options are cheap → buying premium (debit spreads) is more favorable.
Bull Call Spread Oversold + any IV. Buy a lower-strike call, sell a higher-strike call. Defined max loss (the premium paid). Profits if price rises to the upper strike. Best for low/moderate IV.
Put Credit Spread Oversold + high IV. Sell a put at a higher strike, buy a put at a lower strike. Collects premium; profits if price stays above the short strike. Defined max loss is the spread width minus premium received.
Cash-Secured Put Oversold + high IV. Sell a put, set aside cash to buy 100 shares if assigned. Collects premium; you must be willing to own the stock at the strike price.
Call Credit Spread Overbought + high IV. Sell a call at a lower strike, buy a call at a higher strike. Collects premium; profits if price stays below the short strike. Defined max loss.
Bear Put Spread Overbought + any IV. Buy a higher-strike put, sell a lower-strike put. Profits if price falls. Defined max loss (the premium paid).
Defined-Risk First Every structure above has a maximum loss you know before entering. Never sell naked options — the risk is theoretically unlimited. Paper-trade all structures before using real money.
