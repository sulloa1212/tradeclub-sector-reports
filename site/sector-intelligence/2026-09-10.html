7How to Read This Report

A quick decoder for the numbers and labels in this report — methodology lives here, results live in the sections above.

Term What it means
Direction Score (−100 to +100) A weighted composite of trend, relative strength vs SPY, macro tailwinds/headwinds, news/catalyst flow, and momentum/breadth. Positive = net bullish forces; negative = net bearish. Zero = genuinely mixed. Weighted: Trend 30%, Rel. Strength 25%, Macro 20%, News 15%, Momentum 10%.
BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL The one-word label for the sector's net Direction Score reading. Not a buy/sell instruction — it's the current weight-of-evidence tilt for a 3-day–6-week swing horizon.
Conviction: High / Medium / Low How much the evidence agrees. High = most inputs aligned in the same direction. Low = mixed signals, more uncertainty.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) Stock-level score for how likely this specific name is to move hardest in the sector's direction over the swing window. Weighted: Technical setup 35%, Relative strength 25%, Catalyst & news 20%, Move-strength potential 20%. This drives which stocks we select.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) A separate, technically-independent score of the business's underlying health: revenue growth 20%, EPS growth & beat history 20%, margins 15%, FCF & balance sheet 15%, analyst revisions 10%, guidance/backlog 10%, capital returns 10%.
