7How to Read This Report

A quick legend so every number and label makes sense at a glance.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number summarizing trend, relative strength, macro, news, and momentum for a sector. +100 = perfectly bullish across all five inputs. −100 = perfectly bearish. Generated by weighting: Trend 30%, Relative Strength 25%, Macro 20%, News 15%, Momentum 10%.
Conviction: High / Medium / Low
How much all five scoring inputs agree. High = most inputs aligned. Low = mixed signals — the direction may be correct but the setup is murkier.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
A stock-level score for how strongly a single name should move in its sector's direction over a 3-day–6-week swing window. Weighted: Technical setup 35%, Relative strength 25%, Catalyst 20%, Move-strength/beta 20%. This drives stock selection — not the fundamental score.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
