7How to Read This Report

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A weighted composite of 5 inputs: ETF trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth (10%). +80 = strongly bullish; −80 = strongly bearish; near zero = mixed/neutral.
Conviction (High/Med/Low)
How clearly all five inputs point the same direction. High = 4–5 inputs agree. Medium = 3 inputs agree, 2 mixed. Low = inputs are split — be cautious.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Rates the probability a specific STOCK will move hardest in its sector's direction over 3 days–6 weeks. Technically-led: trend alignment (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), beta/liquidity (20%). This drives stock selection.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
