7How to Read This Report

A plain-English guide to every score, meter, and tag on this page — so you never have to guess what a number means.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
How strongly a sector is trending in a bullish or bearish direction. Scored from five inputs: price trend, relative strength vs SPY, macro tailwinds/headwinds, news/catalysts, and momentum. +100 = maximum bullish; −100 = maximum bearish; 0 = no trend either way.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How much evidence aligns behind the Direction Score. High = multiple confirming signals; Low = fewer signals, more uncertainty — treat Low conviction reads as neutral until more data arrives.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Within each sector, how likely this individual stock is to move hardest in the sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing window. Driven by: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst/news (20%), and beta/range potential (20%). Higher = more likely to lead the move.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate score measuring the underlying business quality — revenue growth, EPS growth/beats, margins, free cash flow, balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance, and capital returns. This is independent of the chart. It tells you if the business supports the trade or fights it.
⛽ ADDS FUEL
For longs: Fundamental Health ≥65 — the business quality amplifies the technical setup. For shorts: Health ≤40 — weak fundamentals confirm the bear case. Best when both the chart AND the business agree.
NEUTRAL (fuel tag)
For longs: Health 45–64. For shorts: Health 41–60. Business quality neither adds nor removes edge. Rely more on the technical and catalyst arguments.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND (fuel tag)
For longs: Health <45 — a weak business is a risk to a bullish technical trade. For shorts: Health >60 — a strong business makes the short riskier. The stock may still work technically, but the business pushes against you — reduce size and tighten stops.
⚠ Earnings in Window
The company reports earnings within the 3-day–6-week swing window. This means the trade faces a binary event — a big surprise could gap the stock sharply in either direction. Consider exiting before the report unless you specifically want to hold through earnings (a different risk profile entirely).
BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL tags
Green = bullish directional read for the swing window. Red = bearish. Grey = no clean directional edge. These are swing-trading reads, not long-term investment ratings.
