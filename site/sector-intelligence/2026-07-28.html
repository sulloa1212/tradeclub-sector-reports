7How to Read This Report

A quick legend explaining the numbers, labels, and tags you see throughout.

Direction Score (–100 to +100)

A sector's overall bull/bear lean. Built from 5 inputs: Trend & structure (30%), Relative strength vs SPY (25%), Macro tailwinds (20%), News & catalyst (15%), Momentum/breadth (10%). Positive = bullish lean; negative = bearish lean. The further from zero, the stronger the lean.

Conviction: High / Medium / Low

How confident we are in the direction score, given data quality and signal agreement. High = multiple factors confirm the read. Low = conflicting signals or thin data. Low conviction means the score could flip quickly — be skeptical and size smaller.

Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)

A stock-level score for how likely that stock is to move strongly in the sector's direction over a 3-day–6-week swing horizon. Technically-led: setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), move-strength potential (20%). Higher = stronger setup.

Fundamental Health (0–100)

A separate score for the company's business strength (revenue growth, EPS quality, margins, FCF, balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance). Independent of the chart. Used to grade the ⛽ Fuel tag — does the business support or fight the technical direction?

⛽ Fuel Tag

⛽ ADDS FUEL — fundamentals support the swing direction (Health ≥65 for longs; ≤40 for shorts). NEUTRAL — fundamentals don't help or hurt. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND — fundamentals work against the swing direction (a healthy company is a riskier short; a weak company is a riskier long).

⚠ Earnings-in-Window

An amber ⚠ flag means the company reports earnings inside the 3-day–6-week swing window. Earnings = binary event: a stock can gap ±10–20% overnight. Reduce size, buy a smaller position, or wait until after earnings to enter. Never size a pre-earnings position as you would a normal swing.
