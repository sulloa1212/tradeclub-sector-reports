7How to Read This Report

The legend below explains the methodology behind every score and label. Results and their plain-English reasons are always in the report body above — this section explains how we get there.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)

A composite of five inputs: trend/price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). Positive = bullish signal; negative = bearish. A score near zero = neutral or no clean setup.

Conviction: High / Medium / Low

Reflects how aligned all five inputs are. High = most inputs agree. Low = inputs are split, tape is choppy. When conviction is Low, reduce size or wait for confirmation.

Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)

For individual stocks: how likely is this name to move the hardest in the sector's direction over the 3-day to 6-week swing window? Driven by technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), and move-size potential/beta (20%). Higher = stronger setup.

Fundamental Health Score (0–100)

Absolute business health, independent of the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). Green ≥65, Amber 45–64, Red <45.

⛽ Fuel Tag

⛽ ADDS FUEL = fundamentals reinforce the technical direction (long: health ≥65; short-avoid: health ≤40). NEUTRAL = no strong push either way. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND = fundamentals oppose the direction — adds risk. A healthy company is a riskier short; a weak company is a riskier long.

⚠ Earnings-in-Window Flag

Any earnings date inside the 3-day to 6-week swing horizon is flagged in amber. Earnings = binary event risk: price can gap 10–20% in either direction regardless of the setup. Consider reducing size, using defined-risk options, or waiting until after the print before entering. Always paper trade first.
