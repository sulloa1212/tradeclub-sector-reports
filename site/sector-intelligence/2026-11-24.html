7How to Read This Report

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number summarizing a sector's swing-trade tilt. Calculated from five weighted inputs: price trend (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalysts (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%). +100 = maximum bullish; −100 = maximum bearish. 0 = no edge.
Conviction Level
High: Multiple inputs agree, clear technical confirmation, live catalyst. Medium: Most inputs agree, some mixed signals. Low: Marginal edge only — small position size or wait for better entry.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
How strongly the chart and catalyst support a stock move in its sector's direction, over 3 days–6 weeks. Technically-led: trend alignment (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst (20%), move-strength potential/beta (20%). 80+ = high-conviction setup. 60–79 = solid setup. <60 = lower conviction.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Absolute business quality — independent of the chart. Covers revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance (10%), dividend/buybacks (10%). This tells you how well the fundamentals support or fight the swing thesis.
⛽ Fuel Tag (Longs)
⛽ ADDS FUEL: Health ≥65 — strong fundamentals reinforce the bullish swing. NEUTRAL: Health 45–64 — fundamentals neither help nor hurt. ⚠ FIGHTS TREND: Health <45 — weak fundamentals undermine the bullish case. For short setups, the tags are flipped (a weak fundamental = adds fuel to the bear case).
⚠ Earnings Window Flag
If a stock's next earnings date falls within the swing window (~3 days–6 weeks from today, Nov 24), the earnings date is flagged in amber. An upcoming earnings report introduces binary event risk — the stock can gap sharply either way. Consider reducing size or using defined-risk options strategies around earnings dates.
Color Convention
Green = bullish / long setup. Red = bearish / short or avoid. Amber = caution / watch item. Grey = neutral or no clear edge.
