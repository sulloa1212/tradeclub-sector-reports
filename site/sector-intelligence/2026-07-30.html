Direction Score (−100 … +100) Weighted composite of: trend/price structure (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), momentum/breadth (10%). Higher = more bullish, lower = more bearish. Near 0 = no clean setup.
Conviction (High / Med / Low) How well the five scoring inputs agree with each other. High = multiple factors aligned. Low = mixed signals or thin data.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100) Technically-led score for individual stocks: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & earnings-date proximity (20%), move-strength potential/beta (20%). Drives which stocks we select, not fundamental quality.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100) Separate from the chart: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/buybacks (10%). Green ≥65, amber 45–64, red <45.
⛽ ADDS FUEL For longs: Health ≥65 — strong fundamentals accelerate the technical move. For shorts: Health ≤40 — a weak company is an easier short.
NEUTRAL (Fuel Tag) Fundamentals neither help nor hinder the trade. The setup is driven by price/momentum.
⚠ FIGHTS TREND For longs: Health <45 — weak fundamentals may cap the upside. For shorts: Health >60 — a healthy company is a riskier short; the business itself may resist the breakdown.
⚠ Earnings inside window Earnings are due within the 3-day–6-week swing horizon. The event is a binary wildcard — the stock can gap sharply in either direction regardless of the pre-event thesis. Consider smaller position size or waiting until after the report.
