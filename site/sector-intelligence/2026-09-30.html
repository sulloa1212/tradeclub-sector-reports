Direction Score
A number from −100 (maximum bearish) to +100 (maximum bullish), computed from five weighted inputs: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY over 1–3 months (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth like RSI/MACD (10%). Zero = neutral; ±50 = strong directional read; ±80+ = high-conviction.
Conviction
How confident we are in the Direction Score: High = multiple aligned inputs, clear catalyst, clean trend; Medium = some conflicting signals; Low = choppy, data-dependent. Conviction labels the quality of the setup — not the certainty of the outcome.
Swing-Conviction Score
A 0–100 score for individual stocks: how likely is this stock to move the hardest in its sector's direction over the swing horizon (3 days–6 weeks)? Inputs: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength vs sector & market (25%), catalyst & earnings proximity (20%), move-strength potential/beta (20%). This is technically-led and drives stock selection.
Fundamental Health Score
A separate 0–100 score for business quality, independent of the chart. Inputs: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends/capital returns (10%). This confirms or contradicts the swing call.
