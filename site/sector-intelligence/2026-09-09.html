7How to Read This Report

A quick guide to the numbers and labels you see throughout. The body of the report gives you the results — this legend explains the method.

Direction Score (−100 to +100)
A single number summarizing how bullish or bearish a sector or the overall market looks right now. +100 = as bullish as possible; −100 = as bearish as possible; 0 = perfectly mixed. It is built from five weighted inputs: price trend & structure (30%), relative strength vs. SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%).
Conviction: High / Medium / Low
How confident we are in the directional score, given available evidence. High conviction means multiple inputs are aligned and the signal is clean. Low conviction means the inputs are mixed or data is thin — trade smaller or wait for confirmation.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Applied to individual stocks — how likely is this stock to make the biggest move in its sector's direction over the 3-day-to-6-week swing horizon? Scored on: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength vs. peers (25%), catalyst & news (20%), and move-strength potential / beta (20%). This score drives stock selection.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate, independent score for how healthy the underlying business is — regardless of the chart. Scored on: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), and dividends/capital returns (10%).
