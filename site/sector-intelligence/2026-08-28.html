7How to Read This Report

Short legend explaining the scoring and methodology — the "why" behind the numbers.

Direction Score (−100 … +100)
A composite score for each sector's likely swing-trading direction. +100 = maximum bullish signal; −100 = maximum bearish. Built from 5 weighted inputs: trend & price structure (30%), relative strength vs SPY (25%), macro tailwind/headwind (20%), news & catalyst flow (15%), and momentum/breadth (10%).
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident the scoring model is in the score — driven by how consistently all 5 inputs point the same direction. High = 4–5 inputs aligned. Low = mixed signals.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
For individual stocks — how likely that stock is to move the hardest in its sector's direction over the 3-day–6-week swing window. Technically-led. Built from: technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst & news (20%), move-strength potential / beta (20%). Higher = stronger expected move, NOT "better" investment.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
Completely separate from the swing score — measures business quality (revenue growth, EPS/beat history, margins, FCF & balance sheet, analyst revisions, guidance/backlog, dividends). Independent of the chart.
⛽ Fuel Tags
Longs: Health ≥65 = ⛽ ADDS FUEL (strong biz backs the bullish trade) · 45–64 = NEUTRAL · <45 = ⚠ FIGHTS TREND (weak biz is a headwind for bulls).
