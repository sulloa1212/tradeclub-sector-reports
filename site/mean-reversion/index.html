Key indicator definitions (first use):

  • RSI (14-day Relative Strength Index): a 0–100 momentum gauge — below 30 = oversold, above 70 = overbought, extremes <20 / >80 are severe.
  • Bollinger %B (20,2): measures where price sits relative to its Bollinger Bands — below 0 = outside lower band (oversold), above 1 = outside upper band (overbought).
  • Z-Score: how many standard deviations price is from its 50-day average — beyond ±2.0 is statistically rare.
  • ATR (Average True Range): typical daily price swing size — used to judge whether a move is "big" relative to normal volatility.
  • IV (Implied Volatility): the options market's expectation of future price swings. High IV = expensive options; low IV = cheap options.
  • Composite stretch: our ranking uses RSI + distance from 50/200-day SMA + 1-month % move — names extreme on 3+ measures rank above names extreme only on RSI.

9How to Read / Guardrails

These rules protect you from the most common mean-reversion mistakes.

Reversion vs. Ruin — the most important filter: A low RSI is necessary but NOT sufficient. These patterns disqualify a "reversion" thesis:
  • 🚫 Structural break: fraud, going-concern doubt, failed drug trial, bankruptcy, debt blowup, accounting restatement → RSI is low because the business is broken.
  • 🚫 Catastrophic drop (-50%+ in days) with no stabilization → more likely to continue. CTVA –84% in one month is a flag, not a buy.
  • 🚫 Buyout / merger repricing → stock pins to deal price, not its historical mean.
  • ✅ Keep: moves driven by sentiment/positioning excess over a sound business, ideally near a prior support/resistance level.
Special caveats — always check before trading:
  • ⚠️ Long-vol ETPs (VXX, UVXY, VIXY): only fade when they spike UP. Never buy as "oversold" — structural roll decay grinds them lower over time.
  • ⚠️ Leveraged / futures-based ETFs (UCO, BOIL, UNG, TQQQ, SQQQ, TBT, 2x/3x anything): volatility decay + contango/backwardation roll mean they deviate from any long-run mean. Use for short-term tactical reversion only; for multi-week holds, prefer the unlevered fund or an equity-sector proxy.
  • ⚠️ K-1 tax flag: USO, UNG, UGA, DBC, UCO, BOIL, and most commodity partnerships issue a Schedule K-1. This creates extra tax complexity. Prefer no-K-1 alternatives (PDBC, BCI, COMB) where the exposure allows.
  • ⚠️ Proxy vs. spot: GDX/GDXJ, XOP, COPX, MOO, URA track companies, not the commodity — they carry equity beta and can diverge significantly from the underlying. Named "proxy" in the ETF universe list.
  • ⚠️ Earnings / binary events: A known earnings date inside 30 days can override the technical setup entirely. Always check earnings before entering a mean-reversion trade. Every date in this report is flagged where confirmed by search; unconfirmed dates say "date unconfirmed — verify."
  • ⚠️ Today's 8:30 AM ET data releases: Q3 GDP advance estimate and September core PCE print simultaneously before the open. These are binary events for TLT, IEF, GLD, and rate-sensitive sectors. Wait for the number before entering bond or rate-adjacent trades.
