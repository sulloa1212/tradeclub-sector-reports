A score above +50 = strong bullish; below −50 = strong bearish; near zero = no clean setup.
Conviction (High / Medium / Low)
How confident we are in the direction score. High = multiple inputs agree and the signal is clean. Low = conflicting signals or thin data — tread carefully. When conviction is Low, the score is informational, not actionable on its own.
Swing-Conviction Score (0–100)
Applied to individual stocks. Measures which stock is most likely to move HARDEST in the sector's direction. Weighted toward technical setup quality (35%), relative strength (25%), catalyst proximity (20%), and move-strength potential / beta (20%). This score drives stock SELECTION — not fundamental quality.
Fundamental Health Score (0–100)
A separate, technically-independent score of business quality: revenue growth (20%), EPS growth & beat history (20%), margins (15%), FCF & balance sheet (15%), analyst revisions (10%), guidance/backlog (10%), dividends & capital returns (10%). Green ≥ 65, Amber 45–64, Red < 45.

⚠ Earnings-in-Window flag
An amber warning that the stock's next scheduled earnings announcement falls within the ~3-day–6-week swing horizon.
